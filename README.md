# <img src="public/favicon.svg" width="36" align="center"> Battle Words

A two-player word strategy game. Hide 5 words on your grid, and be the first to uncover your opponent's hidden words.

**[Play on keegant.dev ->](https://battle-words.keegant.dev)**

## How to play

- Share a **room code** with a friend to play together
- Each player secretly places **5 words** (lengths 3-7) on their 10×10 grid
- Take turns **rolling the die** to reveal cells on your opponent's board
- When you think you know all 5 words, skip the die and **Guess All Words** to win

## Setup rules

- One word of each length: **3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 letters**
- Words must be valid English dictionary words
- Words can be placed **horizontally or vertically**
- Words may **cross** each other at a shared letter
- Words that don't cross cannot be directly adjacent

## Die rolls

| Roll  | Action |
|-------|--------|
| 1 - 3 | Reveal **one cell** on your opponent's board |
| 4 - 5 | Reveal a **2×2 area** on your opponent's board |
| 6     | Reveal **every position** of a letter you choose |

## Guessing

At any point on your turn you can skip the die and guess all 5 of your opponent's words at once. A correct guess **wins the game**. A wrong guess **forfeits your turn**.

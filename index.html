BATTLE WORDS

How to Play

Objective

Be the first to correctly guess all 5 of your opponent's hidden words.

Setup

Each player secretly places 5 words on their 10×10 grid - one word of each length: 3, 4, 5, 6, and 7 letters.

  • Words must be valid English dictionary words.
  • Words can be placed horizontally or vertically.
  • Words may cross each other (share a letter at the intersection).
  • Words that don't cross cannot be directly adjacent (touching side-by-side).

Turns

On your turn, either roll the die or attempt to Guess All Words.

1 - 3 Reveal one cell on your opponent's board.
4 - 5 Reveal a 2×2 area on your opponent's board.
6 Reveal every position of a letter you choose.

Guessing

At any point on your turn you can skip the die and guess all 5 of your opponent's words at once. A correct guess wins the game. A wrong guess forfeits your turn.
